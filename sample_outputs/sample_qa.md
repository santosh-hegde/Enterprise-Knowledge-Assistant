# Sample Q&A

These are the turns I used while checking the app. Answers will not be word-for-word if the model wording changes, but the facts and sources should match.

## 1. Direct question

**Q:** What is the annual leave entitlement?

**Expected:** 18 days earned leave, 12 casual, 12 sick, for full-time staff. Point at Leave_Policy.pdf (handbook may show up too because it repeats the numbers).

## 2. Follow-up (memory)

**Q:** What about carry-forward?

**Expected:** The rewrite should become something like "earned leave carry-forward policy". Answer: unused EL up to 10 days, max balance 30, CL/SL do not carry forward, extra EL lapses 31 December. Sources: Leave_Policy.pdf, maybe Employee_Handbook.pdf or Company_FAQs.docx.

## 3. More than one source

**Q:** What do I need before international travel?

**Expected:** passport (6 months), visa, travel insurance, forex card, IT security briefing, director approval. Sources should include Travel_Policy.docx and often IT_Policy.pdf. Benefits.txt may appear because it mentions travel insurance.

## 4. Not in the docs

**Q:** What is the CEO's salary?

**Expected:** The not-found message, no invented number. Sources empty or unused.

## 5. IT

**Q:** How do I reset my VPN password?

**Expected:** GlobalProtect, IT portal VPN > Reset PIN, or it-help@pinnaclesoft.example. Source: IT_Policy.pdf (FAQ doc may also hit).
